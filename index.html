Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Thrive N Design ("we", "us", "our") operates tools and applications that integrate with third-party platforms, including Pinterest. This privacy policy explains how we handle information in connection with those tools.

Information We Collect

Our application accesses your Pinterest account only with your explicit authorization through Pinterest's OAuth process. We access only the data necessary to publish content (pins) to boards you designate. This includes your Pinterest user ID, board information, and pin creation permissions.

How We Use Information

We use the authorized access solely to create and publish pins on your behalf. We do not sell, share, or distribute any account data to third parties.

Data Storage

Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device. We do not store your Pinterest password. No personal data is transmitted to external servers beyond what is required by the Pinterest API to publish content.

Third-Party Services

Our application interacts with the Pinterest API. Your use of Pinterest is governed by Pinterest's Privacy Policy.

Data Deletion

You may revoke access at any time through your Pinterest account settings. Upon revocation, all locally stored tokens are rendered invalid. You may also delete any local data by removing the application from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
